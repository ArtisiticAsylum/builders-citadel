## If a click doesn't work
Clicks need **Reading view** (book icon, top-right), and the links need your vault's name (SETUP step 8 does the find-and-replace). Plain links as fallback: [[North-Star]] · [[Board]] · [[10-Board/Mentors/_index|Mentors]] · [[Executors]] · [[Action-Tracker]] · [[Meta-Map]]

## Update protocol (for any session touching this file)
1. This page is a **drawing of** [[Meta-Map]] and [[Action-Tracker]] — never the source. Update those first, then this.
2. The floor structure is the owner's design and is FIXED once set: Penthouse → Boardroom → Mentors' Gallery → Executors' Workshop → Ground floor + month side-panel. Don't add, remove, or rearrange floors without the owner asking.
3. Small updates only, by targeted edit: the italic lines on each floor, WALL BOARD top-3, project bars, the SLIPPED box, and both "YYYY-MM-DD" stamps.
4. The SLIPPED box only ever states facts from the tracker. Never soften it, never auto-tick anything.
5. **NEVER put a blank line inside the SVG** — Obsidian cuts the drawing at the first blank line. Comments `<!-- -->` are fine.
6. After editing: read back, confirm it ends with `</svg>`, no blank lines inside, renders in Reading view.
