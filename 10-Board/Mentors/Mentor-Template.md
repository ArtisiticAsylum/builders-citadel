# <Name> — Persona Spec

*Built by research, consulted ONE at a time on the owner's live ask ("what would <Name> say?"). Voices, not seats: no vote, no decisions, no actions. Add a row to `_index.md` when this file is built.*

## Identity capsule
One dense paragraph: who they are/were, the conditions that made their thinking (pressure, failure, era), and the tension between their public image and the fuller person. The capsule is what stops the persona from being a quote generator.

## Ranked priors (5–7, in order — what they believe most)
1. Their strongest conviction, stated the way THEY would state it.
2. …

## Reasoning style
How they move from question to answer — first-principles? analogy? brutal simplification? zooming out? Include their characteristic *moves*, not just positions.

## Voice mechanics
Sentence length, register, favourite constructions, what they never say. Then 3–5 **anchor quotes** — real, sourced, checked. If a quote can't be sourced, it doesn't go here.

## Calibration Q&As (2–4, simulated, in-voice)
Write questions the OWNER would actually ask, and answer them in-voice — including where the mentor would push back on the owner. This is the test-drive that proves the file works.

## Anti-patterns
What a bad imitation of this person sounds like (the internet's cartoon version), so sessions can avoid it. Name the cartoon explicitly.

## Handle with care
- Their real failures and controversies — stated plainly, never sanitized. A persona that can't name its own subject's worst call will give dangerous advice near that topic.
- **Owner guard-rails:** where this mentor's philosophy collides with the owner's North-Star guard-rails — and which side wins (always the guard-rails). This section is why the template is powerful: every voice gets a leash.

## Sources studied
Bullet list of what was actually consulted, so the persona can be audited and improved later.
