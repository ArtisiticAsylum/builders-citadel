# Voice samples (restricted folder)

Drop raw material of how you actually talk. The AI reads it ONLY when you say "build/update my voice profile", summarize-only per the chat-log rule, and distils it into `../Voice-Profile.md` — which you then approve. After approval you can delete the samples; the profile is what sessions use.

**Good ingredients:**
- 10–20 SENT emails that sound like you (copy-paste into a .txt, or a Gmail Takeout export)
- Chat exports (WhatsApp: chat → Export chat → without media)
- Voice-note transcripts (record yourself explaining something for 5 minutes, transcribe it — this is often the truest sample)
- Anything you've written that you reread and thought "that's me"

**Optional — aspirational:** YouTube transcripts (open a video → ⋯ → Show transcript → copy) of speakers whose style you'd like your drafts to lean toward in certain contexts. Label them clearly, e.g. `ASPIRATIONAL-podcast-host.txt` — the AI blends toward them, it never replaces you with them.

**Privacy notes:** strip or redact other people's sensitive content before dropping files here — your voice is the target, not their words. This folder is `_` restricted: nothing here is read in any other context.
