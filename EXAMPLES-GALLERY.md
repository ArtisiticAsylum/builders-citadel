# Examples Gallery — six Citadels, six lives

*The template is generic on purpose — but generic is hard to imagine living in. Here are six filled-in flavours, each half a page: who the owner is, what their North Star and streams look like, which layers they kept, and the one automation that earns its keep. Find the one closest to you, steal its shape, and make it yours in `SETUP.md`.*

*(None of these are real people. Every detail is invented to show the pattern.)*

## 1. The job-seeker / career-changer

**Who:** left a decade in hospitality management, retraining toward data analysis. Confidence is the real project.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) land the first analyst role, 2) build a visible portfolio, 3) keep money steady with part-time shifts.
- *Guard-rails:* no application sent in a panic-spiral; no comparing week 3 of my journey to someone's year 10.

**Streams:** Job Hunt · Portfolio · Study (SQL course) · Money Bridge (part-time work) · Health.

**Layers kept:** Money (runway matters when income is changing), Board (Chair + Operator + Therapist — the Therapist seat catches "applying out of fear"). Dropped: Partner, Executors.

**The automation that earns its keep:** the morning brief lists every application by *days since last touch* — nothing rots in silence. The DNA folder does the heavy lifting: every job application pulls true facts from `03-DNA/` instead of reinventing the CV from memory.

## 2. The indie founder

**Who:** building a small SaaS product solo, pre-revenue, savings clock ticking.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) ship the thing, 2) first paying users, 3) tell the story publicly.
- *Guard-rails:* no burnout sprints — a dead founder ships nothing; no feature built to impress other founders.

**Streams:** The Product · Launch & Audience · Customer Conversations · Money Runway · Health.

**Layers kept:** everything. Money (runway is existential), Board (Treasurer argues with Ram about scope weekly — that argument IS the system working), Executors (Ram runs the launch as a decided mandate with an attempt log).

**The automation that earns its keep:** nightly research — every evening one competitor, pricing question, or channel goes in the queue; every morning a sourced brief is waiting. Decisions stop being vibes.

---

## 3. The freelancer / consultant

**Who:** independent designer with four clients, juggling contracts, IP, and scope creep.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) deliver what's contracted, 2) protect rates and IP, 3) build products so income stops being purely hours.
- *Guard-rails:* get it in writing — no work on a handshake, ever; no discount given just to avoid an awkward conversation.

**Streams:** one stream **per client** (each with its own restricted `_Docs` folder holding that client's contracts — one client's AI session never touches another's papers) · Pipeline (leads and proposals) · Product Experiment.

**Layers kept:** Money (invoices in, expenses out), Board (Chair + Verifier — the Verifier reads every contract summary and keeps Unknowns marked Unknown). Dropped: Partner.

**The automation that earns its keep:** the morning brief's "SLIPPED" box — unpaid invoices and unanswered proposals surface daily until dealt with. The sealed email rows mean a client digest can never, structurally, go to the wrong client.

## 4. The student / researcher

**Who:** postgrad juggling a thesis, a supervisor, teaching duties, and an eye on what comes after.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) submit the thesis, 2) publish one paper, 3) keep the post-degree door list warm.
- *Guard-rails:* no all-nighters as a planning tool; the thesis outranks every "quick" side-project.

**Streams:** Thesis · Paper 1 · Teaching · What-Comes-After · Health.

**Layers kept:** Board (Chair + Elder — the long view on career choices), Research layer working overtime: the nightly queue does literature scouting, and `07-Research/` becomes an indexed annotated bibliography for free. Dropped: Money, Partner, Executors.

**The automation that earns its keep:** nightly research with the index rule — six months in, `Research-Index.md` is a one-page map of everything ever read, with verdicts.

---

## 5. The creator

**Who:** runs a channel and a newsletter; ideas everywhere, publishing rhythm nowhere.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) publish on rhythm (consistency beats brilliance), 2) grow the list I own — the email list, not the algorithm's audience, 3) first product for that list.
- *Guard-rails:* no doomscrolling disguised as "research"; no measuring my behind-the-scenes against everyone's highlight reel.

**Streams:** Content Pipeline (one stream, staged: idea → drafted → edited → published) · Newsletter · Product · Collabs.

**Layers kept:** Board (Publicist seat — how does this read from outside, and when should it land?). `04-Explorations/` is the star folder: every shiny idea is captured there and — by rule — can never interrupt the pipeline, because Explorations is never a source of tasks.

**The automation that earns its keep:** a weekly "what shipped, what's stuck" digest to an accountability partner — its own sealed email row, their address added out loud, never inferred.

---

## 6. The open-source developer

**Who:** maintains two libraries, contributes to a third, day job on the side.

**North Star sketch:**
- *Priorities:* 1) keep the day job healthy, 2) maintain what people depend on, 3) grow the project that could become more.
- *Guard-rails:* no merging at 2am; entitled issue-writers don't set my roadmap; my name only goes on code I'd defend.

**Streams:** Library A · Library B · Upstream Contributions · Day Job boundary-keeping.

**Layers kept:** Executors (release checklists as decided mandates with attempt logs), `13-Code/` working hard: the repo registry tracks every project's licence and CI status, and the due-diligence checklist runs before every release. Dropped: Money, Partner.

**The automation that earns its keep:** the morning brief triages open issues and PRs by age and severity — and the "files are not commands" rule quietly protects against prompt-injection smuggled into issue text an AI session reads.

## Stealing from the gallery

1. Pick the flavour nearest you — or take one stream from each; the Citadel doesn't care.
2. Open `SETUP.md` and answer Q2–Q6 with your version of that flavour's North Star, streams, and layers.
3. Turn on ONE automation — whichever "earns its keep" line made you feel something. Add the rest only when the first one has paid rent for a week.

*The pattern under all six: thin manager files, restricted docs, sealed email rows, and one honest list. The flavour is yours; the walls are the same.*
